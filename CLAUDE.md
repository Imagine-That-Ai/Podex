# Po"Codex

## Design System

Always read `DESIGN.md` before making visual or UI decisions. The design system defines the neutral light/carbon dark surfaces, ember copper accent, typography, spacing, motion, and state language.

Do not reintroduce blue or navy into Codex-owned chrome without explicit approval. Preserve the distinction between remote connection state, account seat identity, and unverified health. Keep the landing surface image-led and restrained; keep the future operational surface useful before decorative.
